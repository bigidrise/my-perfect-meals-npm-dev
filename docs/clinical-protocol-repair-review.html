Clinical Protocol Authority & Synchronization Repair

Development draft — paused for safety review; not complete or approved for release.

Clinical blocker: Clinical Labs can recommend metabolic-support, metabolic-stress, and inflammation-support. The inspected live universal-guidance and protocol-envelope paths do not establish corresponding condition-specific guidance for those exact identities. The shadow persistence registry maps inflammation support to Anti-Inflammatory, but that does not establish live guidance authority. These identities were not silently merged with Metabolic Recovery or activated through a shadow-source cutover. An approved clinical interpretation is needed before claiming complete propagation.

What the draft changes

  • Shared compatibility identities normalize Cardiac and Renal aliases while retaining unknown saved values and keeping distinct protocols separate.
  • Lab acceptance checks subject-owned saved measurement evidence and eligibility. Activation and exact removal update protocol state transactionally without changing builders or studio membership assignment.
  • Ordinary nonclinical trainer builder assignments no longer establish physician authority. Explicit physician Oncology and verified assignment evidence remain protected; ambiguous clinical builder assignments require review.
  • Cardiac reassessment requires an active Cardiac protocol and uses qualified language about the measurements assessed, without claiming that a disease resolved.
  • Rejected backend decisions leave the three decision dialogs open with errors and do not invoke success callbacks.
  • A subject-keyed, status-only endpoint and query connect the six builders, professional dashboards, folder, and member status bar without exposing raw measurements.
  • Detailed clinical-data access reuses the established verified-clinician, relationship, organization, and consent policy instead of treating any studio owner as clinically authorized.
  • The existing indicator markup, grouping, and color tokens are retained. No new Pregnancy or GLP-1 indicator was added; the existing physician Hormone Optimization indicator was inspected rather than duplicated.

Verification boundaries

  • Frontend build: passed.
  • Focused protocol, endpoint, Cardiac, and envelope suites: passed in the combined run.
  • Three dialog error/lock paths: six tests passed after correcting the test dialog mock.
  • Broader source-contract checks: two failures remain: the profile-specialty contract expects an inline allowlist and a raw lab-derived call; the ProCare contract expects an older invitation-facade helper name. These checks were not weakened to force a pass.
  • Release type gate: failed with 109 diagnostics versus the reviewed baseline's 109, but a different diagnostic fingerprint. The earlier introduced errors were corrected. Matching counts do not prove an unchanged diagnostic set; review remains required. The baseline was not refreshed or suppressed.
  • No authenticated browser acceptance: the application workflow was not restarted during the safety hold. No claim is made that real profile, builder, trainer, or physician sessions were exercised.
  • No full live lifecycle attestation: join/leave, multi-organization switching, re-login, and complete six-builder generation need remaining acceptance verification. Pure state tests do not establish those flows.

Remaining safety and implementation limits

  1. Resolve the exact intended live treatment of lab recommendation identities lacking established guidance. Do not invent thresholds or merge distinct conditions.
  2. Complete review of per-protocol physician provenance, especially older records with no reliable assignment evidence. The draft policy is not proof that every historical physician restriction can be reconstructed.
  3. Finish review of the Anti-Inflammatory Builder's older primary-mode resolver and mode-dependent generation paths. Shared indicator wiring alone is not proof that every generation entry point uses identical authority.
  4. Finish remaining contract and authenticated acceptance checks, including privacy and subject-switch paths. This work remains part of the original repair, not deferred follow-up scope.

Database and release impact

No schema migration or backfill was run. No production modification, publication, GitHub push, paid AI generation, or clinical threshold change was performed. Verification used deterministic synthetic fixtures and mocked database operations, not live clinical-record mutations. Draft source changes remain in the Development workspace.
