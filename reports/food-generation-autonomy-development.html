Food-generation autonomy: Development report

October 9, 2026. Development only. No GitHub push, publication, subscription changes, Stripe changes, or intentional shared client/account-data changes were performed.

Final automated results: 317 Jest tests passed across 13 suites; zero failures. Development release validation passed all 13 checks, with zero warnings and zero failures. Root TypeScript diagnostics match the unchanged reviewed baseline of 121 diagnostics; this is not a claim that the entire project is free of historical type errors.

Product separation implemented

ExperienceMacro authority
Standalone food toolsDaily remaining allowances no longer establish generation permission in the corrected shared context and validation paths. Preserve requested food, dietary identity, ingredients and independent safety requirements.
My Perfect Menu / Builders / Weekly planningDaily targets remain planning guidance, not rejection ceilings or floors. Chef-linked planning forecasts show available calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat and starch overages without changing the saved targets or counting generation as consumption.
Create with ChefExplicit recipe targets remain separate from daily targets. English requests such as “keep the starchy carbohydrates at 15 grams” and “no more than 15 grams … per serving” are extracted from original user text. Ingredient weights, daily-budget mentions and relative refinements are not converted into absolute nutrient targets.

Chef prompts distinguish nutrient grams from food weight and request actual portion adjustments and recomputation. Existing bounded retries remain. If an otherwise safety-verified recipe misses an explicit target, the response and creation notice disclose its estimated mismatch rather than falsely certifying compliance. Missing requested nutrient evidence remains a verification failure, not a fabricated zero.

Shared corrections

  • Removed daily starch rejection from both shared validators, Low Carb recipe-release budget checks, unified generation filtering/retries and meal-refinement starch correction.
  • Removed daily-derived calorie rejection from GLP-1 and ProCare checks and affected grocery, restaurant, discovery, buffet and refinement paths.
  • GLP-1 remaining fat no longer tightens independently resolved fat tolerability. Diabetic route validation no longer uses the smaller of remaining daily carbohydrates and its existing fixed protocol cap.
  • Corrected shared macro prompts, beverage prompts and carb-cycle prompt wrappers so daily allocations do not become ingredient bans or hard limits.
  • Optional daily-planning resolution failure no longer blocks generation. Independent safety resolution remains separate.
  • Forecasts do not invent planned fibrous-carbohydrate totals: current reservation totals do not retain that category, so unsupported projections are omitted.

Verification and limitations

  • Regression contracts cover zero remaining starch, 50g target / 60g starch food, daily overages across creator-context defaults, recipe-name retention, shellfish-free candidate validation, Vegan/Keto requirements, independent clinical checks, GLP-1 fat authority, carb-cycle guidance and explicit recipe-target parsing.
  • A mocked consumption-route regression confirms actual 800 kcal and 60g starch are stored without clamping to the supplied 500 kcal / 50g daily targets. Tests did not write these values to shared accounts.
  • Missing, blank, boolean, negative and nonfinite required numeric evidence is rejected by the corrected validation paths. This is not certification that every legacy optional-nutrient default throughout the application has been eliminated.
  • The standalone Human Food Context assertion script completed successfully; its initial direct invocation remained alive because of imported database lifecycle work and was stopped by the shell timeout.
  • Earlier failures reflected obsolete strict-starch/budget-error expectations and one introduced forecast typing error; they were corrected. Final tests and release validation have no failures. The reviewed type baseline was not relaxed or changed.
  • The Development workflow was restarted and served the release probes. Browser logs show the app mounted and returned the expected unauthenticated profile response.
  • No signed-in all-surface walkthrough or live AI generation was performed. Candidate-contract fixtures do not prove real model outputs will always retain dish identity, safely adapt recipes or achieve requested nutritional estimates. Release generation probes verified authentication, not recipe quality.
App-wide certification: the shared and audited active paths were corrected, and no ordinary daily-budget rejection is intentionally retained in those paths. I am not certifying every standalone Creator and Builder end-to-end from these tests alone. Unknown composition, unresolved dietary/clinical evidence and impossible safe adaptations may still require review or block a result for reasons independent of daily targets.

Clinical authority retained for review

Existing fixed Diabetes carbohydrate caps, GLP-1 fat tolerability/protein floors and other established diet/clinical safeguards were not indiscriminately removed. Their independence from daily targets was preserved where corrected; their genuine patient-specific medical authority is not established by this engineering test run. Review their provenance before approving a Production release. A condition label, medication status or ordinary daily prescription must not manufacture clinical authority.
