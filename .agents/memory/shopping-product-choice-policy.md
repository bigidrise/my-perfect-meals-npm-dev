---
name: Shopping product choice policy
description: Founder-approved one-click product intent, compact list presentation and identity/safety separation.
---

Choosing an identifiable product is a one-click grocery-shopping action, not medical approval. The founder wants only the chosen brand/product name, normal needed quantity and normal shopping controls on the list. Keep Change/Clear available. Do not put identity expansions, second confirmations, unresolved assessment paragraphs or historical-choice messages in the normal shopping flow.

**Why:** The founder explicitly rejected the multi-step reference workflow and verbose shopping rows as unnecessary complexity.

**How to apply:** Keep original ingredient, quantity, units, recipe provenance, checked state, macros and assessment evidence internally. Comparison cards may show the concise status “Product details not fully verified.” Known allergy, ingredient and clinical conflicts remain blocking.

An exact named product with unspecified package details is different from an unrelated product containing that ingredient. Never substitute buffalo sauce for a displayed avocado-oil product, even if a search returned it under the same brand.

**Why:** The founder's actual avocado-oil option led to a buffalo-sauce catalog record.

**How to apply:** Require reliable identity binding to the displayed brand/product. Missing verification never becomes clinical clearance. A product that cannot be reliably identified remains unavailable rather than receiving an invented identity or a different catalog product. The one-click request does not authorize bypassing safety or identity checks.
