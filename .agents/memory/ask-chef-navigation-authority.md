---
name: Ask Chef navigation authority
description: Founder-approved navigation reliability scope, written-direction authority and release acceptance boundary.
---

Verified application navigation records must govern written directions as well
as buttons. Feature-purpose knowledge is not evidence of a menu relationship.
Explicit named opening requests and clear customer feature selections need no repeated confirmation; educational
questions are not opening consent. Unknown food needs still require contextual
interpretation, not a keyword-to-feature router.

**Why:** A customer received Shopping List directions for Fridge Rescue even
though Fridge Rescue already had an approved independent destination. The
founder approved a focused Development correction rather than rebuilding Chef.

**How to apply:** Keep UI relationships separate from food intent, route
permission and actual workflow execution. A shared-parent explanation may name
the parent without authorizing sibling workflows. Preserve care escalation,
click-time access, destination guards, privacy and concierge-only ownership.

The approval covers Development implementation, beginning with Shopping List,
Biometrics and Daily Journal actions and reviewing the remaining gaps against
actual UI contracts. The separate Create a Dish and Getaway access
inconsistencies are not implicitly approved for correction.

**Why:** The founder approved this staged scope and required review before
Production changes. Paid live-model tests were explicitly left for separate
approval.

**How to apply:** Do not infer release, push, merge, publish, paid test or
Production-workspace authority from this implementation approval. Mocked checks
do not establish live conversational acceptance; dialog features are not
standalone page routes.

Read-only platform education needs its own verified provenance: it is neither a
model-authored food recommendation nor consent to open the described feature.

**Why:** The founder required Meal Builders how-to answers to remain complete
education after output screening discarded useful instructions and recovery
could mistake an unused model field for the source of verified UI guidance.

**How to apply:** Keep reviewed platform explanations distinct from model food
declarations. Do not relax food checks for model content, manufacture an opening
action for education, or treat a generic sign-off as a complete explanation.

Choosing between multiple verified workflows is clarification, not permission to
open one. An unspecified restaurant need must distinguish ordering at a chosen
restaurant from finding somewhere to eat without selecting either destination.

**Why:** The founder explicitly required both restaurant options after screening
substituted an incomplete continuation referring to choices it did not present.

**How to apply:** Validate each replacement as a whole, self-contained reply;
alternatives in rejected fields or earlier history do not complete it. Preserve
safe complete model clarification, but never strip unsafe clauses to rescue a
fragment or let clarification manufacture navigation consent.

An assistant offer is not consent; a customer's explicit feature selection is.
Whole affirmatives bind only the fresh server-owned pending offer, never a
destination recovered from older text or a replacement chosen by the model.

**Why:** Founder transcript review showed repeated permission requests after
explicit My Perfect Menu selections and lost actions after valid confirmations.

**How to apply:** Keep authorization bound to the selected destination and
available tools, after care handling. Attribute reviewed navigation text to its
renderer even when identical to a model field; value equality is not provenance
and must not cause unrelated food-declaration failures.

Offering an Open button is not completed navigation. A fresh, unperformed
handoff remains actionable when the customer confirms again. A clear answer to
the current two-feature choice is a final selection, not another permission
step; an ambiguous yes must not select either alternative.

**Why:** The founder reported Chef saying the customer had already accepted or
been directed to Beverage Creator while the customer was still trying to open
it. The lifecycle must distinguish discussion, recommendation, selection,
action offered and navigation actually performed.

**How to apply:** Bind consent to current server-owned state and its original
age. Only client-observed routing is navigation evidence, not rendering,
authorization success, model claims or conversational acknowledgment. Route
evidence is not proof a destination task completed and grants no entitlement.
Keep evidence owner-, conversation- and original-offer-scoped across preserved
informational copies. Never recover consent from unrelated older prose. Already
lost historical offer state needs a fresh explicit request, not guessed consent.
