---
name: Meal card source labels
description: Source labels identify the originating builder or creator, not the clinical condition.
---

The founder wants meal-card source labels to name where the meal came from. Meals from the Anti-Inflammatory Builder should say Anti-Inflammatory, not Heart Failure.

**Why:** The founder explicitly does not want people seeing “Heart Failure” as the source name alongside labels such as Grocery Coach and Beverage Creator.

**How to apply:** Keep source presentation separate from clinical protocol authority. Changing the displayed source must not change cardiac safeguards or clinical records.
