The founder wants meal-card source labels to name where the meal came from. All builders should follow the Diabetic Hub's behavior when sending meals to Favorites: show the originating builder. Meals from the Anti-Inflammatory Builder should say Anti-Inflammatory Builder, not Heart Failure. Creators should retain their actual creator names.

**Why:** The founder explicitly does not want people seeing “Heart Failure” as the source name alongside labels such as Grocery Coach and Beverage Creator.

**How to apply:** Keep source presentation separate from clinical protocol authority. Changing the displayed source must not change cardiac safeguards or clinical records.
