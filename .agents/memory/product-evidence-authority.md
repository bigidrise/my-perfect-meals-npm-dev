---
name: Product evidence authority
description: Authority boundary for internal packaged-product discovery before customer-facing integration.
---

**Rule:** Treat subject resolution, reviewed product-rule policy, and exact source evidence as separate gates. A condition name, support toggle, legacy provider prompt, or evidence template cannot itself create an executable clinical packaged-product restriction. An exact barcode record observed now establishes source identity, not current formulation; never transfer its observation date to its ingredient or nutrition facts. Unknown fact freshness and rule-specific thresholds remain unresolved rather than receiving convenience defaults. Product Scan's by-name or scanned-label verdict is not a catalog eligibility decision.

**Why:** MPM's established food rules are spread across the envelope, daily planning, Human Food, and shadow clinical authority. A correct evidence reducer can still produce incorrect safety claims if its caller invents who the subject is or what threshold applies. The Human Food Context fingerprint includes a fresh generation-chain ID and timestamps on every resolution; comparing fingerprints from two healthy reads caused a false “profile could not be verified” error. A universal unresolved identity-freshness policy made every Development catalog card say “check the label,” even for people with no active hard rule; exact record identity and formulation age are different questions. USDA publication/retrieval dates do not prove a current package formulation, and its public DEMO_KEY is rate-limited. Open Food Facts search can return irrelevant hits even for a specific query; its index is not package evidence. Product Scan's full-analysis prompt assumes a verified scanned label; catalog ingredients do not satisfy that premise. Development and Production share clinical data, so isolated tests must not be mistaken for live policy approval.

**How to apply:** Resolve the subject again for the actual decision, but never compare separate Human Food Context fingerprints for equality; compare only the safety-relevant search intent built from the first read and use the later resolved subject for all candidate decisions and commentary. Development may recommend a product with freshly checked exact barcode identity and sourced ingredients and nutrition when the resolved subject has no unresolved hard product rule. This is a bounded catalog recommendation, not proof of the current package. A low-carb daily allocation, allergen cross-contact question, or other unreviewed hard rule remains unapproved, with its precise blocker shown rather than a blanket label prompt. Product records must preserve original statements, serving basis, market/variant, source, observation and retrieval dates; AI suggestions and the legacy barcode `verified` boolean do not count as proof. For Open Food Facts fallback, filter index hits against the requested food and US market, then fetch the exact barcode and recheck identity before using any fact. Keep catalog interpretation fact-cited, bound to the exact subject decision, and non-decisional; never pass catalog text as a verified scanned label. Search past rejected or unverifiable candidates within a bounded budget, and never call an unresolved case eligible.
