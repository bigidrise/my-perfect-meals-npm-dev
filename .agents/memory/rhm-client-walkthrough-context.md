RHM plans to distribute its 30-day link to 8,000 clinic clients. Clinic staff want to walk through what those clients will see before distributing it.

**Why:** The user described this audience and the need to see the actual first-time client experience, rather than an administrative experience.

**How to apply:** Keep client-walkthrough preparation separate from clinic-administration access. Do not pre-create the walkthrough account or add special privileges that would change the experience being reviewed.
