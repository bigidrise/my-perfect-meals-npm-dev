---
name: Ask Chef food-screening boundary
description: Why structured proposal checks must not be described as complete chat, allergy or clinical validation.
---

Ask Chef food screening is limited to the model's declared proposed foods, not arbitrary conversation prose. A strict output schema establishes structure, not semantic completeness or real-world ingredient truth. Never describe a no-conflict result as allergy clearance or medical suitability.

**Why:** The user authorized reuse of existing pure food guardrails, but prohibited speculative prose extraction, additional paid model calls, prescription resolution and clinical-permission expansion. Ordinary discussion of a forbidden food must remain possible.

**How to apply:** Keep declarations bound to the response field actually displayed; a rejected offer must not contaminate an independent continuation. Preserve unknown ingredients, allergy status and cross-contact uncertainty. Reuse canonical education with its provenance, without inferring selected priorities or active medical protocols. Mocked verification is not evidence of live model completeness.
