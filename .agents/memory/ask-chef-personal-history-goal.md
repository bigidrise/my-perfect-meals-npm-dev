Long-term personal memory is the founder's number-one Ask Chef intelligence priority. The goal is accurate recall of a person's nutritional journey across months or years, including a nutrition decision made two years earlier, grounded in that person's actual history rather than an AI-generated guess.

**Why:** The founder explicitly wants to know what already exists and can be reused before designing implementation or spending money building anything new.

**How to apply:** Distinguish stored conversations from retrievable historical evidence, considered ideas from implemented changes, and clearing visible conversation from permanently deleting associated memories. The founder has requested investigation of that deletion distinction, not approved changing Clear semantics. Research and feedback are secondary priorities. The audit authorizes findings and recommendations only, not code changes, activation, deployment, or Production access.
