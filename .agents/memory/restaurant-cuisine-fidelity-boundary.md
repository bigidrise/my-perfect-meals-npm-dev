description: Founder review boundary and authority rules for Restaurant Assistant's cuisine correction.

The Restaurant Assistant cuisine correction is authorized in Development only and awaits founder review before any Production promotion. Do not treat source completion or mocked tests as publication, shared-schema restart, live profile modification, or paid-provider-call approval.

**Why:** The founder identified Chinese on Edit Profile as saved cuisine, then authorized a Development-only correction with synthetic profiles, mocked providers/model responses, and preserved clinical protections.

**How to apply:** Keep saved cuisine soft, explicit action cuisine separate and higher priority, restaurant evidence distinct from both, and display language independent. Never invent another restaurant's dishes to satisfy a preference. Explain cuisine conflicts and distinguish old results from results generated under current preferences. Stop if work requires migrations or changes to shared clinical safety contracts.

Structured model cuisine declarations plus bounded contradiction checks do not independently establish culinary authenticity or actual menu availability.

**Why:** A model can mislabel an unfamiliar dish; a cuisine label cannot prove its culinary identity or that a restaurant serves it.

**How to apply:** Preserve uncertainty disclosures, verified-menu item identity, all existing safety checks, and the distinction between fixture verification and live restaurant/model evidence.
