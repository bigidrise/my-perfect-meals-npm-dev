---
name: Low Carb proof scope
description: Why narrow recipe-level Low Carb evidence cannot certify daily source allocation
---

For Low Carb, do not treat ingredient-name classification or model-estimated macro totals as verification of the daily 70/30 carbohydrate food-source allocation. A zero-starchy-source recipe with explicit classifiable ingredients and a resolved subject-owned nutrition budget may be described as compatible with that request, but that is not daily-ratio proof. A starchy dish requires separate day-level source allocation authority before positive compliance; uncertainty is review-required, not a guessed pass.

**Why:** Classification can distinguish cauliflower from starch and reveal ambiguous sauces, but cannot determine how much of a day's carbohydrate comes from each source. A false positive would approve a large pasta dish on names and unverified estimates alone.

**How to apply:** Keep the Macro Calculator's established total carbohydrate target unchanged. Check the actual subject's current budget, ingredient/source evidence, and all ordinary safety rules. Present recipe-level compatibility and daily allocation as different claims in any new food surface.
