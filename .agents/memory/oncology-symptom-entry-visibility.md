---
name: Oncology symptom entry visibility
description: Why consumer oncology symptom selection uses an activation overlay and a below-button review entry.
---

Use a prominent activation overlay for consumer Cancer Support symptom choices, with a visible reopen/summary entry below the condition buttons and before the medical-diagnosis disclaimer.

**Why:** The user reported that the question appearing above the Cancer Support button was hard to notice, even when they knew to look for it.

**How to apply:** Keep the overlay within the consumer tablet frame and usable on desktop, tablet, phone, and short landscape screens. Preserve the existing five symptoms, ownership protections, and parent save/continue behavior; this is a visibility change, not a clinical or persistence change.
