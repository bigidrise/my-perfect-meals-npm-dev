description: Founder-approved offline foundation, automated maintenance requirement, and separate runtime-connection approval.
---

The founder wants automated codebase-wide feature discovery and knowledge
maintenance, not manual identification, description or approval of every feature.
Phase 2 automated enrichment and isolated Development conversation integration
are approved. Enabling it for actual users still requires separate approval.
No Production changes, publishing, database migrations or paid offline enrichment
are authorized.

Phase 3 is authorized only for read-only source analysis, classification,
offline verification and generated reports using the existing infrastructure.
Do not modify application code, routes, navigation, access, clinical protocols,
subscriptions, packages or the separate Surprise Me creator work. Stop after
reporting and wait for founder review before any live integration.

**Why:** The founder explicitly limited Phase 3 to verification and reporting;
source-backed descriptions are not authenticated usability or release approval.

**Why:** The founder explicitly approved building the foundation first while
retaining the live conversation, subscription, clinical and physician boundaries.
Source discovery or an isolated pipeline must not silently turn into live activation.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing feature, capability, navigation and coaching
authorities. Treat discovered labels/routes as structural evidence, not proof of
capabilities or permission. Keep engineering evidence private and report unknown
or stale knowledge honestly. Classify Active/Restricted/Coming Soon/Legacy or
Unused/Unverified before enrichment. Missing dashboard navigation is not evidence
of retirement. Suspected legacy code may be reported, never removed or archived
under this approval. Keep uncertainty and evidence; exclude unverified, internal,
experimental and confirmed legacy functionality from consumer explanations.
Do not add paid offline model calls, runtime source scanning or raw consumer
registry exposure merely because the foundation exists.
