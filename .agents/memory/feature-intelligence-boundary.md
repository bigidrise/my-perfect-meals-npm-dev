---
name: Feature Intelligence approval boundary
description: Founder-approved offline foundation, automated maintenance requirement, and separate runtime-connection approval.
---

The founder wants automated codebase-wide feature discovery and knowledge
maintenance, not manual identification, description or approval of every feature.
The approved foundation is offline and Development-only. Connecting its verified
information to Ask Chef's conversation system requires separate approval.

**Why:** The founder explicitly approved building the foundation first while
retaining the live conversation, subscription, clinical and physician boundaries.
Source discovery must not silently turn into runtime activation.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing feature, capability, navigation and coaching
authorities. Treat discovered labels/routes as structural evidence, not proof of
capabilities or permission. Keep engineering evidence private and report unknown
or stale knowledge honestly. Do not add model calls, runtime scanning or consumer
registry exposure merely because the offline foundation exists.
