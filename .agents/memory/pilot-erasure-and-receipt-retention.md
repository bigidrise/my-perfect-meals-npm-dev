evidence. Erasure must disable redemption without cascading into another

erasure/privacy while retaining necessary financial consent evidence, and
explicitly rejected blindly cascading all related data.

**How to apply:** Keep only the minimal necessary financial snapshot and remove
deleted direct identity references. No specific financial-consent retention
duration has been established. Do not automatically borrow the general audit
log period, invent a purge schedule, or claim complete retention compliance.
Obtain policy-owner approval before automatic purging or live retention claims.
