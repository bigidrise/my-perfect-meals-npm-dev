---
name: Daily Hydration Plan architecture
description: Confirmed modeling principles and implementation gate for MPM hydration intelligence.
---

MPM hydration must be a server-authoritative domain split into immutable intake events, versioned effective daily plan revisions, and recomputable daily state. All features consume this one resolved state; GLP-1, performance, pregnancy, future dysautonomia/POTS, clinician instructions, and preferences contribute typed inputs rather than independent calculators. The consumer Hub has four distinct doors: Everyday Hydration, Athletic Hydration, Sick-Day Hydration, and Liquid Nutrition Support.

**Why:** Separate hydration calculations and browser-owned totals cannot safely resolve restrictions, explain historical behavior, synchronize across devices, or support clinical review. The four-door model keeps ordinary hydration approachable while giving short-term fluid protocols a clear home; it also prevents MPM from presenting an invented liquid diet as medical advice.

**How to apply:** The product owner has affirmed the nonclinical scope lock: one authority, immutable factual history, explicit unknowns, typed plan states, no averaged conflicts, authenticated/auditable access, lossless migration, disabled Phase 1 infrastructure, migration readiness, and dev/prod route parity. Customer education belongs in the App Library, Hydration Hub, and Copilot and must use the customer-facing name “Hydration Hub”; Academy work is out of scope unless explicitly reopened. Liquid Nutrition Support may organize only explicit user- or clinician-supplied instructions into a time-bounded execution checklist/calendar; it must not choose a protocol, invent cadence, infer allowed foods, provide autonomous dosing, count macros, or advance a medical phase. Incomplete, ambiguous, conflicting, or expired instructions fail closed to clarification/escalation. Athletes and sick-day users receive practical, non-prescriptive support rather than autonomous sports-medicine or rehydration dosing. Database provisioning must use the approved migration path rather than schema push tooling, because its checks and append-only triggers are database-only safeguards; do not execute it without explicit authorization. External review blocks activation of individual clinical rules, not generic inactive infrastructure. Preserve every plan revision, policy version, and effective interval; mid-day policy/clinician changes create a superseding revision rather than rewriting history.
