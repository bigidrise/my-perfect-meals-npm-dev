---
name: Independent professional credential clearance
description: Evidence binding, activation separation, erasure and release boundaries for licensed-role credential decisions.
---

Credential clearance is a separate genuine administrative review, never an identity approval, pilot grant, demo exemption or provider activation.

**Why:** Newly approved licensed identities intentionally needed independent clearance, but the staged review system initially had no authorized completion writer. The user authorized only this missing workflow, not account repairs or weakened readiness.

**How to apply:** Require a current authenticated administrator MFA proof, reject self-review and demo-only issuers/subjects, record explicit basis and evidence validity, and retain the append-only decision history. Bind clearance to the approved identity event, approved claims, persisted credential fields and subject security epoch. Changed evidence/identity/security or expired validity requires a fresh review; rejection/pending supersedes prior verification.

Do not bind license validity to Organization selection or the provider activation flag.

**Why:** Those are independent gates; activating a properly reviewed provider must not invalidate the credential decision just obtained. They also never manufacture credential evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve legal, Academy, entitlement, activation, Studio and clinical-access requirements separately. Erasure removes identifying evidence; missing/redacted evidence must fail closed instead of reconstructing approval. Keep the explicit migration and Development-only review/UI release boundaries until separately approved for Production.
