---
name: From Scratch complete recipe package
description: Founder-selected entry, preparation-stage experience, persistence requirements and prerequisite order.
---

From Scratch stays accessible from Recipe Maker, opens its own experience, and feels like Great Outdoors combined with Guided Cooking. Ask only essential questions while respecting the user's saved dietary profile. Produce a preparation guide followed by one finished recipe card, not separate meal cards for each preparation.

**Why:** The founder explicitly selected this product direction and rejected an experience that loses part of what the user created.

**How to apply:** Support as many sequential preparation stages as the recipe requires. Save stage ingredients and quantities, every preparation and cooking instruction, the final recipe and nutrition, image references and the complete Guided Cooking sequence as one reopenable package. Retrieval must not require regeneration. Do not turn alternative outdoor cooking methods into mandatory sequential stages.

Address existing Saved Meals image access first, then Great Outdoors guide persistence, then demonstrated instruction-display defects and serving-percentage issues; only afterward build From Scratch and connect complete-package Guided Cooking.

**Why:** The founder said not to start building From Scratch until saving and retrieval problems are addressed.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing recipes and saved information. Diagnose storage bucket identity, object existence and permissions without assuming permission to widen access or regenerate images on temporary failures. This product decision is not production-release or infrastructure-change authorization.
