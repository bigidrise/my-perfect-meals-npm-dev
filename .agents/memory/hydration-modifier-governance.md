---
name: Hydration modifier governance
description: Durable safety and precedence rules for resolving Hydration context before numeric planning is approved.
---

Hydration contributors emit typed, nonnumeric claims. A single deterministic resolver owns authority precedence, suppression reasons, conflict state, escalation state, provenance, and governance-version consistency.

**Why:** Allowing builders, performance context, condition overlays, or medication context to calculate or overwrite Hydration guidance independently would make clinical restrictions order-dependent and could create unsafe automatic fluid or sodium advice.

**How to apply:** Keep self-reported POTS context-only. Represent clinician POTS protocols as directives without inventing thresholds. Any POTS conflict with organ-safety, fluid, or sodium restrictions must be withheld for review. Preserve every builder contribution independently, reject mixed governance versions, and keep all numeric-plan permission and fields disabled until a later approved phase.
