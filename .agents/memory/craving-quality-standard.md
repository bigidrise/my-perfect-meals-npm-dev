---
name: Craving quality standard
description: Founder-provided standard for category integrity, adapted recipes, variety, and generation efficiency.
---

The standard is: “Did we deliver the food the person asked for, adapted to their needs, using the method they selected?”

**Why:** The founder supplied a focused category-integrity review and warned that earlier food-identity and nutrition-validation work rejected perfectly reasonable recipes.

**How to apply:** Evaluate recognizable recipe structure and actual preparation, not mandatory ingredient words or rigid menus. Adapted vessels and substitutions can preserve identity. Legitimate variations within one category can differ in preparation, texture, defining components, or flavor profile; sharing ingredients alone does not prove duplication. Use existing structured evidence before adding AI verification, and keep ambiguity separate from demonstrated mismatch. Allergy, dietary, clinical, and nutrition safeguards remain authoritative.
