Ask Chef's Grocery Store Coach actions must say **Open Grocery Store Coach**, while the conversation explains which internal function to select, including Find a Product or Create/Build a Meal.

**Why:** The owner identified that a button naming the nested function misleadingly implies navigation opens that function directly. Shopping List is the approved hosted entry point; function selection remains manual.

**How to apply:** Keep intent-specific names separate from navigation-button labels. Preserve validated destinations, access checks and the existing two-stage flow. Do not invent deep links or change Grocery Store Coach to make a misleading label true.

## Diabetic Meal Builder

An explicit request to open the Diabetic Meal Builder must offer **Open Meal Builders** at the approved parent page, explaining that the member opens the builder available to their account there. Do not claim a healthcare professional is universally required.

**Why:** The founder explicitly authorized this parent-page handoff while retaining subscription, clinical assignment and setup safeguards.

**How to apply:** Treat questions about the diabetic builder as education, not navigation consent. Diabetes or a dinner request alone does not select it. Never infer permission to change carbohydrate limits or invent a direct clinical-builder action.

## Concise navigation behavior

Across Ask Chef's supported destinations, identify the destination briefly and
ask whether the member wants to go there. Acceptance gets verified directions
and the working button, with no repeated question or workflow explanation.
Direct opening commands already give permission and skip the offer. Clarify only
when the answer changes the destination; feature explanations are brief and
separate from navigation consent.

**Why:** The founder approved this as standard Ask Chef behavior, not a
Business Suite-only wording correction. Members should reach the destination and
use the workflow already provided there, rather than receive an administration
lecture or support referral in chat.

**How to apply:** Preserve access checks, clinical safety and navigation receipts.
Do not turn this scope into a broad intelligence/knowledge-registry rewrite or
invent navigation destinations that have not been approved.

## Business Suite destination

Business Suite in Ask Chef means **Business Center**, not Organization Hub.

**Why:** The founder corrected the intended destination to `/business-center`;
business discovery should not be sent straight into organization management.

**How to apply:** Keep the Business Suite label and concise handoff, but open
Business Center. Do not infer an organization-management destination from a
general business-starting request.

Business Organization is a separate Ask Chef destination for people who have
already started a business.

**Why:** The founder explicitly asked to add it after correcting Business Suite's
destination; it must not replace the starting point for exploring a business.

**How to apply:** Distinguish starting/exploring from managing an established
business. Keep the same concise offer, acceptance and direct-command behavior,
without assuming ownership or administrator permissions.
