---
name: My Perfect Menu culinary breadth
description: Governing principle for long-run variety across Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snack recommendations.
---

My Perfect Menu uses one shared culinary identity and compact person-scoped history across every eating occasion. Diversity is soft ranking pressure, not a quota or a new nutrition rule. Protein swaps, renamed formats, and cuisine adjectives do not by themselves make structurally repeated food meaningfully different.

**Why:** Exact title/signature uniqueness allowed many technically distinct concepts to collapse into a few repeated bowls, salads, and wraps. Users need Try 3 More to explore a broad culinary universe without fighting their preferences.

**How to apply:** Keep safety, dietary restrictions, explicit intent, cuisine fidelity, and person-specific preferences above breadth. Require structured culinary identity at recommendation boundaries, compare ingredient and structural similarity, preserve compact subject-isolated history, and allow justified repetition when the person's context supports it.
