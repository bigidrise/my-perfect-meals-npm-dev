name: Consumer signup first-run order
description: Regular-user signup should prioritize onboarding rather than trial feature exploration.
---

For basic regular-user signup, the welcome experience should lead into
onboarding and personal meal setup, not feature exploration. Do not treat this
path as Organization onboarding.

The user specified this sequence: Account Created → Consumer Welcome with the
existing required acknowledgements → the large “Let's Get Your Meals Set Up”
introduction automatically at the onboarding entrance → “Start With Step 1”
begins the existing questions.

**Why:** The user explicitly identified the fresh regular-user experience as
important and said the next step should be starting onboarding, not exploring
features.

**How to apply:** Keep trial promotions from diverting or obscuring the welcome
and onboarding path. Preserve required acknowledgements and distinguish trial
feature access from organization identity. Review this flow one step at a time
without broadening it into unrelated organization or billing changes.
Hiding promotions must not remove the seven-day trial, change subscriptions or
expiration, or modify the welcome acknowledgements or onboarding introduction.
