---
name: Professional Hydration role split
description: Authority boundary between clinical Hydration directives and trainer Athletic Hydration coaching.
---

Physicians and authorized clinical professionals may establish provenance-complete, time-bounded measurable Hydration directives. Trainers use a separate nonclinical Athletic Hydration coaching surface for context, before/during/recovery emphasis, reminders, beverage strategy, Creator intent, notes, and review timing.

**Why:** Trainer coaching must remain useful without becoming fluid or electrolyte prescribing or enabling unsafe weight-cut practices.

**How to apply:** Never expose clinical target fields to trainers. Reject trainer content involving fluid dosing, sodium/electrolyte dosing, medical restrictions, water manipulation, sauna, diuretics/laxatives, dehydration, or weigh-in manipulation.
