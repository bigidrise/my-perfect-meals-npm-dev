---
name: Nutrition enforcement classes
description: Distinguish evidence-backed food safety stops from clinical nutrition guidance without inventing universal thresholds.
---

Hard safety applies when an active allergy, dietary identity, stage-specific food-safety rule, clinician restriction, or other established deterministic rule conflicts with known food evidence. Fail closed when an existing high-risk safety policy requires complete ingredient evidence and the source provides only a name or estimate.

**Why:** A health-condition label is not an authoritative patient-specific sodium, potassium, phosphorus, protein, or other numerical limit. Treating every nutrition optimization as an unprovable hard threshold either manufactures clinical authority or makes ordinary food unavailable.

**How to apply:** Preserve specific existing hard stops and authoritative, patient-specific numeric rules where supported. Let Cardiac, Renal, Liver, menopause, thyroid advisory, and other guidance shape food choices without asserting numerical compliance that was not validated. For unknown composition, reject known conflicts, disclose uncertainty where non-hard guidance permits it, and require ingredient evidence for established high-risk profiles. Keep GLP-1 nutrition support distinct from medication status and Diabetes in its dedicated authority path.

Ordinary daily targets and remaining allowances for calories, protein, fat, total carbohydrates, starch, and fibrous carbohydrates never block generation anywhere, including Builders and the Weekly Board.

The user distinguishes three experiences: standalone tools create requested food and report nutrition without daily-budget interference; My Perfect Menu automatically guides planning and shows optional overages; Create with Chef honors explicit numerical recipe-design instructions. Daily targets, actual nutrition, and explicitly requested recipe targets are different authorities.

**Why:** The user approved an app-wide correction superseding both the earlier strict-starch rule and the earlier standalone-only exception. Their subsequent clarification explicitly preserves and strengthens Create with Chef's requested macro precision without making My Perfect Menu a complicated precision tool.

**How to apply:** Separate these sources across resolvers, prompts, repair, and final validation. Never fill explicit recipe targets from a daily profile. Nutrient grams are not ingredient weight; calculate per-serving contributions and disclose uncertain or unmatched estimates. Keep the original recipe unless the person requests adjustment. Creation/save is not consumption, and actual logged food may exceed daily goals. Genuine safety authorities remain independent; ambiguous clinical rules require review, not automatic removal. This records product policy, not proof of complete implementation.
