---
name: Nutrition enforcement classes
description: Distinguish evidence-backed food safety stops from clinical nutrition guidance without inventing universal thresholds.
---

Hard safety applies when an active allergy, dietary identity, stage-specific food-safety rule, clinician restriction, or other established deterministic rule conflicts with known food evidence. Fail closed when an existing high-risk safety policy requires complete ingredient evidence and the source provides only a name or estimate.

**Why:** A health-condition label is not an authoritative patient-specific sodium, potassium, phosphorus, protein, or other numerical limit. Treating every nutrition optimization as an unprovable hard threshold either manufactures clinical authority or makes ordinary food unavailable.

**How to apply:** Preserve specific existing hard stops and authoritative, patient-specific numeric rules where supported. Let Cardiac, Renal, Liver, menopause, thyroid advisory, and other guidance shape food choices without asserting numerical compliance that was not validated. For unknown composition, reject known conflicts, disclose uncertainty where non-hard guidance permits it, and require ingredient evidence for established high-risk profiles. Keep GLP-1 nutrition support distinct from medication status and Diabetes in its dedicated authority path.

Ordinary daily calorie, protein, total-fat, and total-carbohydrate goals must guide food choices and track overages, not block meals.

Macro Calculator numbers and daily planning allocations, including remaining starch, belong to meal planning in the Builders. They must not govern whether someone can create a standalone recipe, dessert, craving, beverage, Grocery Coach meal, or restaurant choice. The user chooses whether to eat, log, or plan the food; honest nutrition reporting remains useful without making it permission to create food.

**Why:** The user clarified: "the only place I really want that macro calculator numbers ... to matter is in meal planning ... with the meal builders" and "builders and only builders." This supersedes the earlier instruction to enforce remaining starch across all food surfaces.

**How to apply:** Separate Builder planning authority from standalone food creation in shared context resolution, prompts, generation, repair, and final validation. A shared resolver must not make every Creator depend on today's remaining budget. Genuine allergies, dietary choices, and patient-specific clinical restrictions are separate authorities; this clarification is not authorization to remove them. Never infer a clinical hard maximum from a daily goal or a condition label. Preserve dish identity and honest nutrition, and distinguish planned forecasts from consumed intake. This is the requested product boundary, not a claim that the application has already implemented the separation.
