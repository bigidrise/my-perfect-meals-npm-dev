---
name: Hydration Center strict paywall
description: Defines the purchased-plan boundary for My Perfect Hydration Center.
---

My Perfect Hydration Center requires a purchased Pro-or-higher plan. Free and Essential accounts must see the upgrade modal and remain blocked even when global pre-launch, tester, founder, sandbox, or trial bypasses grant broader app access. Basic water logging remains free.

**Why:** Internal access bypasses made a Free dummy patient appear entitled in production testing, preventing the customer-facing paywall from being exercised.

**How to apply:** Use the strict purchased-plan entitlement boundary for the Hydration Center route, its entry CTA, and advanced personal hydration APIs. Do not apply it to the free water-log API or weaken professional hydration safety and authorization gates.
