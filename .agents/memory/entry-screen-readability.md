---
name: Entry-screen readability
description: Founder requirements for readable first-time entry and onboarding without replacing the Hub text-size controls.
---

My Perfect Meals is designed for adults of different ages and visual abilities. First-time visitors need readable supporting text before they discover the Hub's text-size setting.

Preserve the existing A / A+ / A++ controls and Standard, Large and Extra Large preferences. A readability correction must work with those preferences, not reset, replace or override them.

Preserve the dark orange-and-black branding, large headings, layout identity and primary buttons. Supporting descriptions, instructions and important explanations should be readable at approximately 16px on mobile with regular or medium weight, rather than making everything bold.

**Why:** The founder explicitly approved targeted entry/onboarding improvements because supporting text was too small, while emphasizing that the existing Hub controls must not be disturbed.

**How to apply:** Keep entry/onboarding typography changes scoped; check all three preferences, including narrow mobile reflow, normal-text contrast and password-control touch areas. The approval for this work was Development-only and did not authorize changes to authentication, consent, onboarding decisions, subscription logic, clinical safeguards or Production.
