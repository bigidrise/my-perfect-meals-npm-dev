---
name: Copilot pause policy
description: Founder-requested pause of Copilot and its voice features in My Perfect Meals.
---

The founder authorized hiding and disabling all active legacy Chef Copilot
narration and listening controls, including welcome/onboarding introductions,
restaurant and nearby-meal tools, calculators, guided cooking, Kitchen Network,
Organization Success Center, Academy, certification, and narration settings.
Do not translate legacy narration scripts.

**Why:** The founder explicitly approved platform-wide reversible narration
retirement after the earlier voice-dependency audit.

**How to apply:** Treat this as a reversible Copilot pause, not permission to
delete components, scripts, provider accounts, credentials or unrelated Studio
media. Preserve written education and disclaimers, cooking timers and progression,
certification completion, microphones/transcription, Ask Chef, and tutorial
videos. Hidden narration must not preload, play, or initiate paid TTS requests.
Work belongs in the Development workspace; publication and Production changes
require separate explicit approval. Do not expand this work into localization.
