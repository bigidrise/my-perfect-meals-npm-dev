name: Getaway real-world dining
description: Practical restaurant advice, profile-driven cuisine flexibility, and incomplete ingredient evidence.
---

My Perfect Getaway is a practical dining assistant, not a recipe-verification gate for kitchens the platform does not control. Automatically use the customer's saved nutrition profile; do not add diet or cuisine dropdowns.

Cuisine preferences are flexible. Required dietary patterns are not: a vegan customer must not receive chicken or fish as a fallback. Search the actual destination and selected area, distinguish menu evidence from restaurant listings, and explain when no matching cuisine can be confirmed. Search failures and incomplete coverage cannot establish that the venue has no matching food.

Missing full restaurant ingredients alone should not reject every reasonable suggestion under optional Anti-Inflammatory support. Provide practical choices with explicit preparation/ingredient uncertainty, without claiming validated compliance. Known ingredient conflicts, allergies and medically necessary restrictions remain protected.

**Why:** The founder said restaurant ingredients are outside our control and customers still need a healthy something to eat. Their supplied guidance distinguishes flexible cuisine preference from required dietary restrictions and asks us to reuse Restaurant Assistant and Find Meals Near Me rather than invent conflicting dietary rules.

**How to apply:** Keep the optional-support uncertainty policy specific to unverified Getaway dining advice. Do not weaken shared recipe, product, allergy or medical validators. Reuse existing restaurant-menu providers and dietary checks. Never fabricate menu inventory, ingredients, allergy safety, or clinical compliance.
