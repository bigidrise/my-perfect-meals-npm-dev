---
name: Anti-Inflammatory food concept matching
description: Preserve prohibited prepared-food concepts without banning their ordinary component ingredients.
---

A prohibited prepared food does not make each word or whole-food component of its name prohibited. Matching corrections must preserve the existing nutrition policy, including genuinely prohibited dishes and unresolved unqualified materials; they never waive allergies, clinical authority, cultural rules, or dietary identity.

**Why:** The founder supplied a verified generation failure in which ordinary onion was rejected as onion rings because a blocked phrase was allowed to match a shorter ingredient name in reverse.

**How to apply:** Keep ingredient descriptions intact, distinguish whole foods from complete prohibited concepts, and test the real generation final scan and Menu adapter as well as the matching helper. Do not interpret this correction as permission to remove prohibited foods or infer that ambiguous oil/grain/dairy evidence establishes compliance.
