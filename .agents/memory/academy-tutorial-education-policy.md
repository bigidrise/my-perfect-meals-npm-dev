---
name: Academy tutorial education policy
description: Founder-approved tutorial rollout and replacement of automatic instructional popups.
---

Feature education uses consistent near-top tutorial play buttons. Preserve correct
existing YouTube links; do not invent links or assign an unrelated tutorial.
Missing links stay hidden or disabled until the founder supplies them. Athlete's
Beverage Creator uses its founder-supplied dedicated tutorial.

**Why:** The founder supplied a platform-wide Academy tutorial rollout brief and
wants to connect videos one feature at a time.

**How to apply:** Record each feature-to-video mapping, verify the exact target
and mobile/desktop behavior, and report completion before the next assignment.
Pending URLs do not authorize generic fallback videos.

On phones, prioritize a compact tutorial play icon while preserving the full
44×44 tap target and accessible tutorial name; desktop may retain the caption.
Do not squeeze chat controls or change modal scrolling to fit tutorial controls.

**Why:** The founder explicitly required every tutorial control to fit the
smaller space available on mobile.

**How to apply:** Use the shared responsive tutorial control, allow crowded
heading rows to wrap, and verify narrow-screen geometry without shrinking taps.

Use one founder-supplied Training Nutrition Schedule tutorial across all four
schedules (General, Diabetic, GLP-1, Anti-Inflammatory), Performance Hub, and
Performance Builder.

**Why:** The founder said the five-minute video covers all Performance pieces,
including initial setup, updating setup, and meals.

**How to apply:** Use the same comprehensive video for the schedules and
Performance Hub/Builder; do not request separate Performance tutorials.

For the current rollout, skip Pregnancy, Beginnings, Pets (Dogs and Cats), and
wine-related tools until the founder resumes them; those videos are not finished.
Leave other existing builder videos unchanged while collecting Lifestyle videos
one feature at a time.

**Why:** The founder explicitly excluded these unfinished tutorials and asked to
supply the remaining Lifestyle links individually.

**How to apply:** Ask for one eligible feature's link, connect it and confirm,
then ask for the next; do not repeatedly request deferred videos.

Automatic instructional Copilot cards and Quick Tour introductions are disabled
reversibly. Preserve explanatory content, stored preferences, manual Chef/Guide
help, Ask Chef, safety/medical alerts, required consent and transactional messages.
This supersedes older guidance saying disabling automatic Copilot education was
only under consideration.

**Why:** YouTube Academy tutorials are now the primary feature-education experience.

Embedded instructional Pro Tip cards across the meal builders are also hidden
under the centralized instructional visibility policy; retain their underlying
content. Guest welcome education must follow the same automatic-open policy.

**Why:** The founder explicitly approved finishing both omissions while preserving
manual Copilot voice, chat, help, and Chef buttons.

**How to apply:** Classify triggers before changing them. Gate only automatic
educational presentation, never required operational work or user-requested help.
Verify in Development without unnecessary paid AI calls; publication and release
still follow the existing approval gates.

The smiley Chef buttons in the bottom navigation and left sidebar must open
`/coach-corner/home`, not trigger instructional Copilot cards.

**Why:** The founder clarified that pressing either navigation smiley should open
the actual Ask Chef page, not a card. This overrides retaining card triggers on
those two navigation controls; it does not request disabling voice or chat.

**How to apply:** Keep these navigation buttons independent of Copilot opening
and explanation timers; preserve the destination page's existing access checks.
