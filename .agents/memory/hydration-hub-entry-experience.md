---
name: Hydration Hub entry experience
description: Product constraints for immediate Hub rendering and visible Creator handoffs.
---

Hydration Hub must render its shell and four-door selector immediately instead of blocking the whole page on account-specific data. Today, history, preferences, directives, protocols, and personalization may hydrate behind that shell and load independently.

**Why:** A noticeable full-page startup spinner makes the Hub feel like it must boot every time it opens and hides the primary decision the user came to make.

**How to apply:** Keep safe last-known data visible during refreshes where available, never show invented numeric or clinical data while waiting, and isolate slow or failed secondary sections. Everyday and Athletic doors must retain obvious, customer-facing Creator actions that use the signed, account-bound handoff; Creator still re-resolves current safety context.
