---
name: Hydration Hub entry experience
description: Product constraints for immediate Hub rendering and visible Creator handoffs.
---

Hydration Hub must render its shell and four-door selector immediately instead of blocking the whole page on account-specific data. Today, history, preferences, directives, protocols, and personalization may hydrate behind that shell and load independently.

**Why:** A noticeable full-page startup spinner makes the Hub feel like it must boot every time it opens and hides the primary decision the user came to make.

**How to apply:** Keep safe last-known data visible during refreshes where available, never show invented numeric or clinical data while waiting, and isolate slow or failed secondary sections. Everyday and Athletic doors must retain obvious, customer-facing Creator actions that use the signed, account-bound handoff; Creator still re-resolves current safety context.

Hydration Center should be a prominent consumer Dashboard feature, not treated as merely a Biometrics water tracker.

**Why:** The user considers its full support experience too valuable to remain buried in Biometrics and approved a compact Dashboard entry rather than duplicating the entire interface.

**How to apply:** Preserve the full-page Center and its access rules. The user authorized moving only Basic Hydration Tracking from Biometrics to the top of the Center page and removing its old surrounding card and open-center button.

The hydration tracker relocation must not change plan access, paywalls, or logging behavior.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected a proposed access change: “I said move this to there that's it.”

**How to apply:** Treat this as placement-only work; do not expand it into entitlement or hydration feature changes.

Barrier selection must not make Everyday beverage creation appear unavailable.

**Why:** The user expected drink creation while using Temperature or Forgetting strategies and interpreted the absence of a Create card as loss of access.

**How to apply:** Keep a direct signed Creator entry near the practical options, independent of which barriers produce recipe cards. Distinguish saving setup from requesting options in loading feedback; saving must not imply that options were requested.
