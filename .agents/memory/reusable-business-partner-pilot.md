name: Reusable business partner pilot
description: User-requested open distribution of the founder Business Pilot, distinct from individual invitations and organization offers.
---

The user needs one reusable Business Pilot link that any business partner can use with their own existing or new account. One partner's activation must not consume the shared link. Each account receives its own original 30-day clock, and returning to the link must not renew that clock.

**Why:** The user distributed the earlier individual invitation to other partners and found that its email-bound, one-recipient behavior did not meet their distribution needs.

**How to apply:** Keep reusable partner enrollment separate from email-bound private invitations and organization/location-owned Business Offers. Preserve existing account plans, billing, and professional credential requirements. This work remains Development-only until separately approved for release.

Do not introduce forwarding restrictions now. The user said, “We'll worry about that as we grow.”

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritized a working reusable link over safeguards against sharing it.

**How to apply:** Treat forwarding controls as deferred scope, not a prerequisite for current partner enrollment. Do not imply that a public reusable URL authenticates or verifies someone as a practitioner.
