---
name: Reusable business partner pilot
description: User-requested open distribution of the founder Business Pilot, distinct from individual invitations and organization offers.
---

The user needs one reusable Business Pilot link that any business partner can use with their own existing or new account. One partner's activation must not consume the shared link. Each account receives its own original 30-day clock, and returning to the link must not renew that clock.

**Why:** The user distributed the earlier individual invitation to other partners and found that its email-bound, one-recipient behavior did not meet their distribution needs.

**How to apply:** Keep reusable partner enrollment separate from email-bound private invitations and organization/location-owned Business Offers. Preserve existing account plans, billing, and professional credential requirements. This work remains Development-only until separately approved for release.

Do not introduce forwarding restrictions now. The user said, “We'll worry about that as we grow.”

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritized a working reusable link over safeguards against sharing it.

**How to apply:** Treat forwarding controls as deferred scope, not a prerequisite for current partner enrollment. Do not imply that a public reusable URL authenticates or verifies someone as a practitioner.

The Business Pilot is one complete experience: personal Clinical access, including Essential, Pro and Hydration capabilities, plus the appropriate Business workspace and role-appropriate Studio/client management. Existing accounts, expired personal trials and lower paid personal subscriptions must not reduce the granted experience. New personal and organization pilot access share one 30-day period beginning at successful authorized activation.

**Why:** The user clarified that this is the link they give businesses to evaluate the whole product, not an organization-only trial requiring a separate personal purchase.

**How to apply:** Ordinary sign-in does not activate the offer. Preserve purchased subscriptions, professional verification, MFA, readiness, consent, tenant isolation, paused features and affiliate restrictions. Never reset an existing organization's pilot to repair missing personal access; historical reconciliation requires separate, auditable approval and the original expiration.

Customer invitations belong on the customer-facing Production app, not the Development preview or its separately published Development deployment. The bare sign-in URL is not a pilot link.

**Why:** The user clarified that real participants are meant to use Production even though product development happens in this workspace.

**How to apply:** Verify the destination and activation behavior in the actual serving app before distributing a reusable link. A Development-only campaign or a link on the Development hostname does not become a Production invitation by sharing it with customers.
