description: Governs ambiguous savory-side presentation and bounded image recovery.
---

**Rule:** Ingredient-confirmed ambiguous savory sides receive plated savory guidance; after two validator failures, only eligible meals get one simplified recovery image, and it must explicitly PASS before storage.

**Why:** Creamy mash wording can produce dessert vessels, while broad recovery can damage soups, bowls, drinks, and desserts. Substring category checks also misclassified “flank” as flan and “steak” as tea.

**How to apply:** Use whole-word category matching, treat recipe ingredients as side authority, preserve strict validation, cap generation at three attempts, and send every failed or skipped recovery to the semantic placeholder without persistence.
