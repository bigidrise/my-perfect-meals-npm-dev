---
name: Private Pilot ProCare
description: Rules for temporary founder-controlled professional and client access without changing commercial subscription identity.
---

Private Pilot ProCare is an explicit, audited, time-bounded entitlement. It must never be represented by writing a fake Stripe plan key or inferred from Clinical, affiliate, role, or certification state.

**Why:** The private program must support strategic pilots while preserving the public subscription model and keeping consumer Clinical separate from professional Studio access.

**How to apply:** Compute the entitlement at runtime. Continue enforcing professional role, readiness, training, MFA, and current legal acceptance. Provider-linked client access must remain seat-limited and stop when its provider grant expires or is revoked.
