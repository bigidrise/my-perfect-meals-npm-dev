name: Optional personal nutrition support freeze
description: Product decision to pause optional personal GLP-1 and Anti-Inflammatory support stacking without dismantling established meal safety.
---

Optional self-selected GLP-1 and Anti-Inflammatory support overlays are paused while the product emphasizes established Builders. Preserve their saved source rows, history, and architecture for resumption. This pause is not permission to remove established Builder behavior, medical/clinical activation, or food safety protections.

**Why:** A hidden switch with an active saved row would still alter meals unexpectedly. The product's near-term focus is making established Builder workflows reliable for coaches and clinics, rather than expanding optional stacking.

**How to apply:** Keep the UI and meal-authority reader boundary aligned. Do not treat old/global preference badges as clinical evidence or make them imply paused preferences affect generation. When resuming overlays, review both boundaries together and test existing saved choices before exposing the controls again.
