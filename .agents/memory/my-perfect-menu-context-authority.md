---
name: My Perfect Menu context authority
description: Safety and subject-isolation rules for saved concepts and Weekly Board handoff.
---

Saved concept sets are valid only for the exact server-resolved food authority that generated that category. Use an opaque, deterministic, per-category context stamp and recheck it immediately before full meal generation. Missing diabetes data may offer the canonical Diabetes Hub update path but is not a new medical gate; only existing approved escalation rules may interrupt generation.

Household plans use an authorized server-derived board namespace. A browser-provided household identifier is only a request to resolve the active owned subject; it is never a repository key or authorization proof.

**Why:** A saved concept can become unsafe after dietary, allergy, protocol, diabetes, GLP-1, preference, or subject changes. Owner-default board storage also caused meals generated for a household member to appear on the wrong board.

**How to apply:** Stamp each category after successful governed generation, treat legacy or mismatched stamps as stale, and rerun current authority before selection. Resolve household board scope from the authenticated owner plus active owned profile, and propagate that subject through reads, writes, cache partitions, events, and navigation.
