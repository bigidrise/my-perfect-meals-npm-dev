---
name: Icon pill choices
description: Visual convention for compact icon-based selectors across meal creators and Gatherings.
---

Icon-based food and cooking choices use a small standalone icon above a compact pill containing the option name. Pills size naturally to their labels and wrap on narrow screens.

**Why:** Large fixed-width icon-only buttons with separate labels feel oversized and inconsistent with the shared compact pill controls.

**How to apply:** Compose the shared pill control through the icon-pill option wrapper for cooking methods and similar illustrated choices. Do not enlarge or globally alter the base pill component.
