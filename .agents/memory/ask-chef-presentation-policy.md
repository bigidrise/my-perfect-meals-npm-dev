---
name: Ask Chef presentation policy
description: Quiet internal safeguards, relevant customer explanations and bold verified feature names.
---

For Ask Chef, internal guardrails should operate quietly. Customer-facing
explanations should appear only when relevant to the customer's actual request.
Ordinary feature navigation and education must not announce allergy status,
ingredient verification, cross-contact or internal screening.

**Why:** The founder explicitly distinguished asking which feature makes a
protein shake from requesting an allergy assessment. Removing irrelevant
commentary does not authorize removing any underlying protection.

**How to apply:** Preserve clinical, allergy, dietary, privacy, subscription and
navigation checks. Never hide a warning while retaining the food advice that
required it; any reviewed UI replacement must discard the entire model draft.
Explicit safety questions and genuine food recommendations retain appropriate,
accurate safety communication. Do not guarantee allergen or medical safety.

Make verified feature names bold, not yellow or green. Use the authoritative
registry, not invented feature names, raw Markdown markers or new navigation
permissions. Keep direct handoffs concise.

**Why:** The founder chose bold as clean, accessible emphasis consistent with
the product's existing visual identity, without distracting colors.

**How to apply:** Check actual rendered emphasis and mobile wrapping separately
from authenticated destination clicks; component fixtures do not prove a
signed-in end-to-end handoff. This approval is Development-only, with no release.
