---
name: Hydration product recommendation boundary
description: Governance order for any future commercial or partner product surfaced from Hydration.
---

Future product recommendations must be downstream of hydration context or an authorized plan, a practical need, eligibility, verified composition, and user-safety checks.

**Why:** Commercial relationships must never determine clinical, nutritional, or physiological eligibility. Phase 1 intentionally contains no product, supplement, affiliate, formula, wearable, or athletic-cutting recommendations.

**How to apply:** Qualify a product independently first and preserve provenance plus the qualification reason. Only after that may availability, sponsorship, affiliate disclosure, or other commercial metadata affect presentation.
