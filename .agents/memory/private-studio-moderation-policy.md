---
name: Private Studio moderation policy
description: Delivery and audit rules for moderate profanity in authenticated private Studio communication.
---

Authenticated private Studio text, voice, and video messages may deliver when ordinary/moderate profanity is the only moderation finding. Preserve the sender's original text or transcript verbatim, retain a moderation flag internally, and keep all other existing blocked categories enforceable.

**Why:** A completed private professional communication should not be lost solely due to ordinary language, but the safety controls for threats, contact sharing, off-platform communication, grooming, and misconduct remain necessary.

**How to apply:** Use the private Studio moderation policy only on authenticated professional-to-client and client-to-professional Studio channels. Keep the base moderator unchanged for public or community surfaces, and never overwrite the authoritative transcript with a polished variant.
