## Review scope naming

Instruction-consolidation approval does not authorize the separate conversational-memory roadmap.

**Why:** The user called the Development-only instruction-consolidation review "Phase 2", while the older roadmap uses that name for conversational memory. Phase names alone are ambiguous.

**How to apply:** Follow the explicitly approved scope, not its phase number. Preserve the isolated synthetic review harness; real member-history retrieval, persistent conversational memory, paid reviews and Production activation require their own approval.
